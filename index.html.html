Business English — Speaking Practice

Explaining a revised forecast

Situation: A country manager has submitted a lower full-year forecast. Explain the impact to the Board and suggest the next steps.

Speak aloud: Give a 45-second update as if you were in a Board meeting.

Useful vocabulary

Revised outlook — herziene verwachting
Underlying assumptions — onderliggende aannames
Downside risk — neerwaarts risico
Mitigation measures — beheersmaatregelen

Geen schrijfopdrachten. Spreek je antwoord hardop uit. Voor interactieve gesproken feedback kun je ChatGPT Voice gebruiken.
